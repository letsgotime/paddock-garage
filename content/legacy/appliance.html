What you notice in the first week

  • Full torque from a stop, cold or not: electric motors don't need to warm up the way a gas engine does. The power is there the instant you press the pedal, even on the coldest morning of the year.
  • The cabin is ready before you are: start the climate from the app and it's already comfortable by the time you're in the seat, no idling in the driveway first.
  • One-pedal driving: regenerative braking handles most of the slowing down in stop-and-go traffic, and it's part of why the physical brake pads last so much longer than a gas car's.
  • Updates show up on their own: new capability can arrive overnight with no shop visit.
  • Real cargo room: 34.3 cubic feet behind the rear seats, 76.2 with them folded flat, plus a separate 4.1 cubic foot frunk up front. Enough to move a mattress or a week of gear without a roof box.

What other owners end up telling you

  • The frunk has a drain plug. It's a documented feature, not a workaround: pull it and the frunk can be hosed out, or packed with ice as a cooler on a road trip.
  • Controls → Charging has a "Keep Accessory Power On" toggle. Switch it on and the 12V and USB outlets stay powered, down to 20% battery, without Sentry Mode needing to run at all. Useful for a dashcam or a cooler overnight.
  • Camp Mode turns the back seat into somewhere to sleep. Fold the seats flat, run Camp Mode, and it holds cabin temperature and keeps the interior lit off the battery instead of a running engine.
  • Dog Mode is real, and most non-owners don't believe it. Leave the dog, and the AC keeps running while the center screen shows the cabin temperature for anyone who walks by and worries.
  • Windows crack open from the app. On a hot day, you can vent the car before you're anywhere near it, without fully unlocking it.
None of this is unique to this specific car: it's what the Long Range AWD platform does for anyone driving one, legacy or Juniper. What makes it feel like an appliance instead of a car is that most of it runs in the background, without being asked.
