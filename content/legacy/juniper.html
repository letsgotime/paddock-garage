Tesla's refreshed "Juniper" Model Y arrived for the 2025 model year, unveiled January 2025, U.S. deliveries starting March 8, 2025. Your car is a pre-refresh 2024, and the differences are concrete, not just cosmetic buzzwords:

What Juniper changed

  • Full-width LED light bars front and rear, replacing your car's separate headlight/taillight units.
  • New rear-seat touchscreen (8") for climate/media. Your car has no second screen for rear passengers.
  • Removed the gear-selector stalk: Park/Drive/Reverse moved to the touchscreen, similar to Tesla's Model 3 redesign.
  • Kept the physical turn-signal stalk, unlike the Model 3 redesign that eliminated it entirely. Your car and Juniper both still use a real stalk for turn signals.
  • Acoustic glass front and rear for lower wind noise; retuned suspension bushings reviewers describe as noticeably more settled over expansion joints.
  • EPA range rose to roughly 340+ miles, mostly from aerodynamics and efficiency work rather than a bigger battery.
One thing that did not change at launch, worth knowing: Juniper launched with the same 15.4" center screen size your car already has. A larger 16" QHD screen didn't arrive until a June 2026 running change, rolled out gradually, so plenty of early Juniper cars share your exact screen size and resolution.

Charging hardware, NACS port, and Supercharger network access are unchanged between the two generations. Nothing about how you charge today is "outdated."
