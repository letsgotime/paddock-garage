Long Range AWD sits almost exactly in the middle on price, and it's the trim Edmunds' own comparison recommends for most buyers: "plenty of range and more than enough performance." What separates the trims beyond the motor count:

  • Wheels & suspension: Performance rides on 21" wheels only and gets a lowered suspension tune and bigger brakes; your car uses the standard ride height on 19" or 20" wheels.
  • Third row: A 7-seat option was available on Long Range AWD in 2024 (priced at $2,000 as of April 2024) before being dropped entirely at the 2025 refresh.
  • Top speed: 135 mph on your car vs. 155 mph on Performance.
  • Cosmetic only: Performance adds a rear spoiler and aluminum pedals. Otherwise the trims look nearly identical from the outside.
