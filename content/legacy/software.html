What this car has, confirmed

  • Autopilot (Autosteer + Traffic-Aware Cruise Control) is standard equipment on every Model Y, including this one, at no extra cost.
  • This car is running FSD (Supervised) v14.3.6, and the $99/month subscription is active on the current owner's account: they added it themselves, so this isn't a leftover trial from a previous owner. "Supervised" is Tesla's own term, meaning the system drives but the driver is required to stay attentive and ready to take over; it is not an unsupervised/robotaxi-grade system on this hardware.
  • The car is also subscribed to Premium Connectivity ($9.99/month, or $99/year): the paid tier above Standard Connectivity's free-for-8-years baseline. It adds satellite-view maps, live traffic visualization, in-car video and music streaming (Netflix, Disney+, Spotify and the like, each still needs its own separate app subscription), an in-car web browser, weather radar, and live Sentry Mode camera viewing from the app when away from the car. It renews next on September 21, 2026.
  • Phone Key is set up: the owner's phone unlocks and starts the car over Bluetooth automatically on approach, no card tap needed. Worth keeping a physical Key Card in the glovebox or a wallet anyway, as backup for the rare time a phone's dead or Bluetooth misbehaves.
  • Software build 2026.20.6.11 is what this car is currently on.
  • Real usage, not a subscription sitting idle: per the Tesla app's Self-Driving Stats, this car is running 81% self-driving on recent trips (64 of the last 80 miles) with a longest single FSD streak of 21.4 miles and a 2-day streak currently active.
Worth knowing for whenever you sell: your FSD subscription is tied to your Tesla account, not the VIN, so it doesn't transfer with the car. If you sell, the subscription simply ends. A buyer who wants FSD starts their own $99/month plan. That's a real difference from the old one-time-purchase model, which Tesla ended entirely on February 14, 2026: FSD bought outright before that cutoff stays with the car, VIN-tied, permanently. Premium Connectivity works the same way: it's a subscription on your account, not an asset that conveys with a sale.

This profile doesn't pull live telemetry: no real-time Autopilot mileage, streaks, or charging history. Those live inside the owner's Tesla account and the mobile app; nothing here claims to sync with them.
