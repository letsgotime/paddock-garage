NHTSA's official decode agrees

We pulled the same VIN through NHTSA's free public vPIC decoder, the U.S. government's own database, independent of anything Tesla or we calculated by hand. It confirms Make (Tesla), Model (Model Y), Model Year (2024), Plant (Fremont, California, USA), and drive configuration (Dual Motor): a clean match on every field it covers, plus a passing check-digit validation, meaning this VIN is well-formed and untampered.

One gap, not a disagreement: NHTSA's database doesn't carry Tesla-specific detail like battery pack size or Long Range vs. Performance trim. That's a known limitation of the public database, not an error. Trim confirmation (Long Range specifically) comes from your Tesla account and window sticker, not NHTSA.
