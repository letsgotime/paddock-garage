PADDOCK MOBILE · 2024 Model Y Long Range AWD · Solid Black

The #20 wrap

Racing livery over the factory Solid Black paint. The brand’s slanted bars become the stripe package at car scale, rising front-to-rear on both sides. Number 20 on both front doors, URL on the rockers and tailgate, scannable QR on the rear quarter glass. Black paint does most of the work, so the wrap is a partial: stripes, number, type, and marks only.

Full-wrap program · three directions blue base · black only where needed

Directive: full coverage, Paddock20 blue as the base, factory black visible only where it should be (glass, pillars, trim, camera housings, wheels). All three keep the same brand constants: #20 door roundels, rocker URL both sides, tagline and QR at the rear, real type cut in vinyl, never printed by a generator.

Why partial coverage wins here. The car is already Solid Black, which is the livery’s base color. A full wrap re-covers black with black for thousands more. This design installs as stripes, number kits, and lettering: a day in the shop, and any panel is replaceable in minutes. QR minimum size 8 inches: tested scannable at 10 to 15 ft when parked. Nothing covers lights, glass forward of the B-pillar, plate, or camera housings.

The file you upload to the car

tesla-wallpaper-1920x1200.png · sized for the Model Y center display

What Tesla actually accepts. Tesla does not take custom wrap files that re-skin the 3D car model; the on-screen car color comes from Toybox ’Paint Shop’ presets. What you can upload is a custom wallpaper: this file, via the touchscreen (Toybox → Wallpaper → add from USB) or from your phone’s gallery through the app. The physical wrap above is what a shop cuts from this spec; the wallpaper is the livery living on your screen either way.
