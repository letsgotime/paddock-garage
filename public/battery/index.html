Warranty, the good news

The battery and drive unit are covered to 2032 or 120,000 miles, whichever comes first, with a floor of 70% capacity retention. At 104 cycles and 0% measured loss, that floor is a long way off. The basic vehicle warranty runs to 2028 or 50,000 miles.
