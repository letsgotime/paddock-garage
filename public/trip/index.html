The eighty per cent rule, in one comparison

At Florence the car charged twice, back to back, on the same plug.

32% to 79%

33.7 kWh in 22.3 minutes, peaking at 164 kW.

79% to 95%

11.6 kWh in 19.9 minutes, peaking at 56 kW.

Nearly the same time on the clock. A third of the energy. measured on 2026-09-04. This is why a road trip stops at 80% and drives on.
